GoTripJ: Group Trip Planner

[Short description] (max 80 characters)
Plan trips together, then turn your trip into a photo book and rolling paper.

[Full description]
GoTripJ is a group trip planner for families, friends and couples, made for the planners in every group. Everyone sees the same itinerary, and everyone can add to the memories.

■ Plan your itinerary
· Day-by-day schedule with done and hold
· Paste an itinerary from an AI assistant and it fills in at once
· One tap opens Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps for each drive
· Places you skipped wait in the Hold box until you have time

■ A shared itinerary for the whole group
· Join with one invite link — no sign-up
· Edit the schedule, budget and packing checklist together
· Different plans at the same time: kids' club for the kids, pool for the adults
· Owner, participant and view-only roles
· Works offline, even on a cruise ship, and syncs when you're back online

■ Turn the trip into a photo book
· Add photos, a one-line mood and a diary entry to each stop (private or shared)
· Get an 8×8 trip photo book PDF when you're home
· Her & His couple photo book: the same places through two pairs of eyes
· Rolling paper: everyone leaves a note for each person, decorated with stickers

■ Great for
· Family trips, couple trips and group trips that need a shared itinerary
· Keeping a travel budget and packing checklist together
· Cruise itineraries and road trips
· Anyone who wants a trip photo book when they get home

■ Private by design
· No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
· Photos and private notes stay on your phone
